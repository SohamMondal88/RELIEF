Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Relief Web App is a demonstration project. Do not enter real patient information. This page describes what the code actually does. The full list is in docs/PRIVACY_AND_DATA.md.

1. What stays in your browser

Relief has no server of its own. What you enter is saved in your browser (local storage) on this device and is not sent to Relief:

  • Demo account details (name, email and a hashed password) and sign-in sessions.
  • Medicine reminders and the dashboard timeline.
  • Appointment requests (name, email, phone, notes). A request is only a saved record. It is not sent to a doctor.
  • Saved facilities, the doctor you picked, your language, and a saved location (rounded to about 1 km, kept for 1 day from GPS or 7 days if typed).

The Relief Assistant chat and symptom checker answers are not saved.

2. What Relief does not do

  • No cookies, no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts.
  • No sale of data, because Relief does not receive any.
  • No encryption of what is kept in your browser. Anyone who can use your browser profile can read it.

3. Other websites that see you

Pages load fonts, icons, map code, map tiles and some images from other hosts (Google Fonts, Font Awesome, unpkg, OpenStreetMap, Google, and a few image hosts). They can see your IP address. If you type a place that is not a built-in town into the manual location form, the text is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. The SOS buttons open your phone or SMS app, and the message goes through your carrier. The contact form does not send anything.

4. Your choices and deleting your data

  • See what is stored, and delete your account and all Relief data in this browser, in Your data and account on the dashboard.
  • Deleting removes local data at once and cannot be undone. If a deployment connects an online sign-in service, deletion also asks that service to delete your account, and tells you plainly if it could not.
  • Change location permission in your browser at any time.

5. Changes to this policy

This page changes with the code. The date above is the last time it was checked against the code.

6. Questions

Open an issue on the Relief repository. Please do not put personal details in it.
