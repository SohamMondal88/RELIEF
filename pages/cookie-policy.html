Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how Relief Web App uses cookies and similar technologies. See the privacy policy for everything else.

1. Cookies

Relief does not set any cookies. It counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies. It does not use Google Analytics.

2. Local storage

Relief keeps your sign-in session, reminders, saved facilities, language and saved location in your browser's local storage instead. It stays on your device. You can delete it from Your data and account on the dashboard or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

3. Third parties

Fonts, icons, maps and some images are loaded from other websites (for example Google Fonts, Font Awesome, unpkg and OpenStreetMap). Those sites may set their own cookies. Relief does not control them. Block third-party cookies in your browser settings if you do not want them.

4. Changes to this policy

This page changes with the code.

5. Questions

Open an issue on the Relief repository. Please do not put personal details in it.
