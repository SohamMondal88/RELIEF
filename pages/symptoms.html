Immediate Medical Attention Recommended

Your responses indicate potential emergency warning signs that require prompt evaluation by medical professionals.

What to do right now:

  • Contact your local emergency number (such as 911 in the US/Canada, 112 in India/Europe, or 999 in the UK) immediately.
  • Go to the nearest hospital emergency department or urgent care center.
  • Do not drive yourself if you are feeling faint, confused, or in severe distress; call an ambulance or have someone transport you.
  • Do not wait or delay emergency care. Routine self-care advice does not apply to potential emergencies.

Important Medical Understanding & Clinical Limitations

  • Not a Medical Diagnosis: This tool provides general health education only. It does not identify diseases or provide confirmed diagnoses.
  • Multiple Possible Causes: Many illnesses share similar symptoms. Only a qualified healthcare professional who examines you in person can determine the cause.
  • Professional Assessment: If your symptoms persist, worsen, cause concern, or do not improve, consult a qualified doctor or healthcare provider.
  • Prescription Safety: Never start, stop, or adjust prescription medications or dosages without direction from your prescribing physician.
  • Safety Reassurance Warning: The absence of listed warning signs does not guarantee you are safe. If you feel very unwell, seek professional medical care promptly.
