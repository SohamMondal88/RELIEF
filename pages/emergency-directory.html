Emergency resource directory

In a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number now. RELIEF does not dispatch help and does not call for you. Tapping a number opens your phone's dialer, and nothing is dialled until you press call there.

Search by service, number, region or word, for example "ambulance", "UK" or a number you were given. Every helpline shows its source and when it was last checked, or says that no check is recorded. This page works without signing in and without sharing your location.
