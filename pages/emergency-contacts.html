Emergency numbers by region

In a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number now. RELIEF does not dispatch help and does not call for you. Tapping a number opens your phone's dialer, and nothing is dialled until you press call there.

Each number below shows where it comes from and when a person last checked it. A number that has not been checked, or was checked a long time ago, is marked so you know to confirm it. This page works without signing in and without sharing your location.
